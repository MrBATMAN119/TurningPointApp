Our Mission & Beliefs

"Your word is a lamp to my feet and a light to my path." - Psalm 119:105

Torah Observant: We believe in keeping the biblical commandments as given in Scripture.

Biblical Feast Days: We celebrate God's appointed times as outlined in Leviticus 23.

Clean Living: We follow biblical dietary laws, avoiding "the piggy" and unclean foods.

Hebrew Roots: We understand our Christian faith within its Jewish foundation.

Street Evangelism: We actively share the Gospel in our community.

Messiah Yeshua: We follow Jesus within the framework of Torah observance.
